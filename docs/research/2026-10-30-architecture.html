Research decisions · checked 30 October 2026

Durable execution first. Optimize against measured outcomes.

Two entry points share one execution substrate: direct vendor ACP and an Auto task orchestrator. Preserve vendor sign-ins and native caches. Keep optional services behind explicit interfaces.

Recent evaluation guidance

  • MemoryArena, September 2026 revision: test memory across interdependent sessions, not recall alone.
  • Total Cost of Agency, September 2026: account for injected memory and context accumulation, not only generated output.
  • Same Context, Different Agents, October 2026: context techniques interact with agent behaviour; combinations need evaluation.

First gate: deterministic correctness, recovery and application-overhead benchmarks without paid calls. Next: small held-out model tasks, then repeated larger projects. Keep discovery trials separate from confirmation.
