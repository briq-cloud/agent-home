OpenRouter batch: bounded adapter review

Research, implementation and small live probe · 30 Oct 2026

What this supports: opt-in asynchronous summarize, extract, or rewrite operations over caller-supplied text, with a persistent job handle. It does not route CLI/ACP prompts or interactive agent decisions. The adapter requires per-job paid consent and a caller cost estimate. That estimate is visible intent, not a billing ceiling.

Unit tests use an injected fake BatchClient. The installed SDK is @openrouter/sdk 1.5.21. A separate staging probe used synthetic text and the home-owned OpenRouter key; the key stayed on the home.

Small live probe

Two fact-extraction inputs on Haiku 5.5, Anthropic backend: synchronous $0.0000808, 2.8 seconds total; batch $0.0000554, about 14 minutes. Both retained the required facts (2/2). Token counts and cache state differ. This is a functional/billing check, not an agent-quality or general discount result.

Integration

The service exposes text-batch job endpoints behind RUNNER_BATCH=1, off by default. Jobs need per-job paid consent and idempotency keys. Background polling resumes persisted provider IDs; ambiguous POSTs never resubmit. CLI sessions and typed agent decisions keep their existing paths. Provider jobs may take 24 hours and currently cannot be cancelled.
